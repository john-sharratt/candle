# The Maker's Repertoire

## Every task a Maker can be set, in any world it is attached to

480 tasks in 71 clusters, 23 currencies. Written at the grain of an *errand with a
judgement in it* — never a mechanism, never a branch — and designed to **chain**:
each cluster declares what it needs and what it leaves behind, so a mission is a
path through them and a strategy is a choice of path.

The hierarchy is **goal ← mission ← tasks**. A goal is what the world wants; a
mission is one route to it; a strategy is the reasoning that picked this route
over another, and the thing that turns a mission into a task list.

This document is authored into the world as a section collection. It is the
specification of what a Maker's life contains — not of what the engine can
currently execute. See [Engine notes](#engine-notes) for the difference.

---

## Invariants

Eight rules every task holds to. They are what the list is checked against, and a
task that breaks one is wrong however good it reads.

1. **A task is an errand with a judgement in it.** One purpose, one thing the
   Maker has to decide for itself. Not a mechanism, not a branch, not a sequence
   of moves. *Find someone who looks like they will listen* is a judgement; *if
   nobody is in the room, move to the next room* is a flowchart with a task's
   name on it.

2. **A task is never a tool with a new name.** If it can be satisfied by one tool
   call, it is a tool call. A task is above the tools and spends several of them,
   in an order the Maker works out.

3. **The model fills the gaps, and it is an engine fault when it cannot.** Tasks
   assume a character clever enough to cross a room, find a person, and pick a
   moment. Nothing here spells that out. A Maker stuck on the mechanics is a
   defect in the engine or the design, never a missing task.

4. **No proper nouns. Every task names a role.** The standing list of what is
   unwritten; whoever holds the neighbouring span; wherever drafts are read
   aloud. The world supplies the nouns. A Maker attached to a world five minutes
   old must be able to take any task here.

5. **Every task declares what it needs and what it leaves.** Tasks are composed
   into missions, so they must join. A cluster's signature — `finding → claim` —
   is the joint. A task that consumes nothing and produces nothing is an island
   and does not belong.

6. **You can tell when it is done.** Borrowed from the craft: if you could not
   photograph the moment a task is satisfied, it is not a task, it is a mood.
   *Understand the era better* fails; *find the two entries that cannot both be
   true* passes.

7. **Name the act, never the motion.** Measured, not assumed: an instruction
   containing a motion verb produced nineteen moves out of nineteen turns and no
   conversation. Tasks name what is being *done* — tell, ask, judge, refuse,
   file — and leave the walking to the Maker.

8. **Completeness before feasibility.** A task with no tool behind it yet is a
   task the engine has still to grow, and is kept.

### The grain, by example

| Not a task | A task |
|---|---|
| Move to an empty area. Approach someone in the room. Say something to them. If nobody is in the room… | Find someone nearby who looks like they will listen, and tell them about your last creation. |
| The tool list with new names on it. It spells out the mechanism and it branches, so it is doing the model's thinking badly. A character that cannot work out how to cross a room is a fault in the engine. | One purpose, one judgement, a reference to the character's own history, no instruction on how to get there. Two Makers given this will do it differently, which is the point. |

### Marks

- **Two** — cannot be done alone
- **Think** — raises deliberation
- **Seeds** — creates work for others
- `needs → leaves` — what a cluster chains from and to

## Roles, not proper nouns

A Maker has no world of its own and is always working in somebody else's, so
nothing here may name a setting. Every task names a *role* — the standing list of
what is unwritten, whoever holds the neighbouring span, wherever drafts are read
aloud — and the world supplies the noun. The same discipline the identity anchor
already keeps:

> Deliberately world-agnostic. A Maker is spawned into a world and edits that
> world, so nothing here names a setting, a span of years, a cast or an event.
> Where the anchor would have said "2461 to 3087" it says "whatever the world
> says", because the same file has to be right for a world six centuries old and
> for one opened five minutes ago with nothing in it.
>
> — `personalities/maker.yaml`

The *building* is common ground and may be named: every world a Maker works in
has a Creator's Vault, with a level for the record, one for its people, one for
its places, one for its images and one where orders sit. What a given world calls
its spans, its fixtures and its cast is the world's to say.

## Currencies

What passes between tasks. A task that leaves `claim` can be followed by any task
that needs `claim` — which is what turns a list into a graph, and a path through
the graph into a mission. Three families: what the work produces, what happens
between Makers, and what a Maker forms in itself.

| Currency | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `·` | nothing needed — an opener, always available |
| `finding` | something noticed that could become work |
| `claim` | a thing held, and the responsibility for it |
| `grounding` | enough context to act without contradicting |
| `plan` | the thing broken into pieces, in an order |
| `draft` | made, not yet accepted |
| `verdict` | somebody's judgement of a made thing |
| `accord` | a thing settled between two who both hold part of it |
| `filed` | now part of the record |
| `wake` | the consequences of a change, needing repair |
| `attention` | somebody is listening to you now |
| `bond` | a relationship warmer than it was |
| `rift` | a relationship damaged |
| `debt` | an obligation owed, in either direction |
| `promise` | a commitment with a time on it |
| `standing` | how the cast rates you at something |
| `view` | an opinion you have actually formed |
| `resolve` | a commitment made to yourself |
| `question` | an enquiry taken from outside, not yet answered |
| `custody` | a thing formally held, with its origin written |
| `description` | a written way in to something too large to read |
| `condition` | a judgement of how well something has survived |
| `energy` | what a Maker carries out of an encounter |

## Worked missions

Six chains, to show the composition. Four of the six pass through social clusters
— not as decoration, but because the work does not complete without them.

### Close the longest silence
*Goal: the record has fewer holes in it.*

1. Look over the standing list of what is unwritten and take the one you would most regret leaving. — *Noticing*
2. Take it, knowing it is now yours. — *Claiming*
3. Read far enough either side that you could be contradicted and would know it. — *Grounding*
4. Break it into pieces you could finish one at a time. — *Planning*
5. Write it, and keep writing until it stops being a summary. — *Making*
6. Carry it to wherever people sit and read it to whoever will listen. — *Testing*
7. Take the objection seriously enough to change something, or work out why you will not. — *Testing*
8. File it, and let the list get one shorter. — *Filing*

### Settle a boundary that has been sitting
*Goal: two neighbouring spans of the record stop contradicting each other.*

1. Find two entries that cannot both be true, and work out which the rest depends on. — *Noticing*
2. Decide what you actually think, having been describing it neutrally. — *Forming a view*
3. Find out where the other holder stands on a question you will have to settle. — *Alliance*
4. Find whoever holds the span next to yours and agree where the two meet. — *Settling*
5. Write down why it was settled that way, for whoever asks in a year. — *Settling*

### Make a change the record will survive
*Goal: a thing everyone knows is wrong gets fixed without breaking six other things.*

1. Find something you wrote that you now think is wrong. — *Maintaining*
2. Work out what depends on it, before you change it. — *Change & wake*
3. Say so to somebody before you change it. — *Maintaining*
4. Make the change, and go and warn everyone whose work it breaks. — *Change & wake*
5. Go round and fix the things your own change broke. — *Change & wake*

### Bring a new Maker in
*Goal: somebody who arrived yesterday is useful and not alone.*

1. Welcome a Maker you have not met and tell them one thing they will need. — *Belonging*
2. Take them round the building and hand them to the people they will need. — *Arrivals*
3. Tell them the one rule of this place that is not written down anywhere. — *Norms*
4. Give them real work and stay near enough to catch it. — *Teaching*
5. Correct what they did so they can do it themselves next time. — *Teaching*

### Mend a working relationship
*Goal: two Makers who need each other can work together again.*

1. Work out why you have been avoiding a particular piece of work. — *Self-knowledge*
2. Speak first to somebody you have not properly spoken to in a long time. — *Long estrangement*
3. Admit you were wrong, to the person you were wrong at. — *Repair*
4. Do a favour you owe without mentioning that you owed it. — *Repair*
5. Ask them to work on a thing with you rather than beside you. — *Alliance*

### Become the one who knows this best
*Goal: the cast has somewhere to send a question it currently has nowhere to send.*

1. Decide to become the one here who knows a particular thing best. — *Resolve*
2. Follow a thing that catches your interest with no idea whether it will be useful. — *Attention*
3. Copy out a passage somebody else wrote well, to find out how it is made. — *Practice*
4. Explain how a part of this building works that they have never had to touch. — *Asking & telling*
5. Write down how you do a thing, so the next Maker need not work it out. — *How work is done*

## Social — 217 tasks

The full repertoire of what people do to each other, not the subset an assistant
performs for a user. The clusters most often missing from NPC design — and most
obvious by their absence — are *repair*, *rivalry*, *withholding*, *norms*,
*interruption* and *play*: without them a cast can only cooperate, and a cast
that can only cooperate is scenery that agrees.

### Arriving & leaving — `· → attention`

- Come into a room where people are working and let them know you are there without breaking whatever they are in the middle of. **[Two]**
- Notice who is here and greet the one you have not spoken to in longest. **[Two]**
- Say what you have come for, so nobody has to guess whether you want something. **[Two]**
- Leave properly — tell whoever you have been working near that you are going, and where. **[Two]**
- Find the person you meant to speak to before you leave, though it would be easier to go. **[Two]**
- Come back to somebody you left mid-conversation and finish it. **[Two]**
- Arrive somewhere you were not expected and make it clear you are not checking up on anyone. **[Two]**
- Leave without saying anything, and know it will be noticed. *(leaves rift)*

### Idle company — `attention → bond`

Contact carrying almost no information and doing nearly all the bonding.

- Sit down near somebody working and get on with your own thing beside them. **[Two]**
- Say something small and true about the room you are both in. **[Two]**
- Let a silence run rather than filling it, and see whether the other one fills it. **[Two]**
- Look at the same thing as somebody else and say what you see in it that they might not. **[Two]**
- Stay longer than you needed to, because leaving now would say something you do not mean. **[Two]**
- Complain lightly about something neither of you can change. **[Two]**
- Remark on the weather of this place — the light, the quiet, how full it is today. **[Two]**
- Work in the same room as somebody all day and exchange almost nothing, on purpose. **[Two]**

### Curiosity about a person — `attention → bond, view`

- Find out what somebody is actually interested in, as against what they were assigned. **[Two]**
- Ask somebody what the best thing they ever made was, and get them to say why. **[Two]**
- Work out what somebody is avoiding, and decide whether to say so. **[Two] [Think]**
- Ask what somebody would work on if nobody ever asked them for anything. **[Two]**
- Find out how somebody decides a thing is finished, because you suspect they do it differently. **[Two]**
- Notice something about how somebody works that they may not have noticed, and tell them. **[Two]**
- Ask somebody what they were like when they arrived here. **[Two]**
- Find out what somebody is proud of that nobody has praised. **[Two]**

### Listening — `attention → view, bond`

- Hear somebody out to the end without preparing your answer while they are still talking. **[Two]**
- Say back what you understood, in your own words, and let them correct it. **[Two]**
- Ask the second question, the one that only makes sense after the first answer. **[Two]**
- Notice the thing somebody said quickly and moved past, and go back to it. **[Two] [Think]**
- Listen to two people talking and work out what neither of them is saying. **[Think]**
- Admit halfway through that you stopped following, and ask them to go back. **[Two]**

### Asking & telling — `attention → grounding, standing`

- Ask somebody something you genuinely cannot answer yourself, and keep asking until you understand it well enough to argue with. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody what you have concluded, not what you have been doing. **[Two]**
- Explain how a part of this building works that they have never had to touch. **[Two]**
- Warn somebody about a thing you got wrong, before they reach the same place. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Check a thing you believe against somebody who would know, and be willing to lose the belief. **[Two]**
- Pass on something you were told that the person in front of you needs and does not have. **[Two]**
- Answer a question you were asked earlier and could not answer at the time. **[Two]**
- Ask a question you suspect has an uncomfortable answer. **[Two]**

### Opinion & disagreement — `draft, view → verdict`

- Say exactly what is wrong with what you just heard, and do not soften it into nothing. **[Two] [Think]**
- Find the one person whose opinion would actually change your mind, and get it. **[Two]**
- Disagree with a colleague's entry, naming the entry rather than the person. **[Two] [Think]**
- Say the thing you have been not saying because it would complicate the work. **[Two]**
- Take an objection seriously enough to change something, or work out why you will not. **[Think]**
- Hold your position against somebody who outranks you on this subject. **[Two] [Think]**
- Agree with somebody out loud and specifically, so agreement is on the record too. **[Two]**
- Notice you have both been meaning different things by the same word, and settle it. **[Two] [Think]**
- Give an opinion you know will be unwelcome, to the person least likely to want it. **[Two]**
- Say you have no view on something, rather than manufacturing one. **[Two]**

### Persuasion & bargaining — `view → accord, debt`

- Work out what would persuade this particular person, and try that rather than what persuaded you. **[Two] [Think]**
- Offer somebody something they want in exchange for the thing you need. **[Two]**
- Concede the smaller point to win the larger one. **[Two] [Think]**
- Make the case for a thing you only half believe, because the work needs it decided. **[Two] [Think]**
- Get somebody to say out loud what it would take to change their mind. **[Two]**
- Stop arguing and simply ask them to do it because you are asking. **[Two]**

### Promises & coordination — `plan → promise`

The only cluster that creates obligation outliving a conversation — which is what
lets a mission span more than one meeting.

- Promise somebody a thing by a time, knowing you will be held to it. **[Two]**
- Agree with somebody who does which half, so neither of you does both. **[Two] [Think]**
- Arrange to meet somebody later, somewhere, about something. **[Two]**
- Remind somebody of a thing they said they would do, without making it an accusation. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody you will not make the time you promised, before the time. **[Two]**
- Do the thing you said you would, at the time you said, without being reminded.
- Ask somebody to hold a thing for you until you get back. **[Two]**

### Standing — `filed → standing`

Status among peers with no formal hierarchy — earned by what you have made and
what you know, contested constantly.

- Defer to somebody who has worked this part of the record far longer than you. **[Two]**
- Say plainly that you know this subject better, when it matters that they believe you. **[Two]**
- Credit somebody else's work in front of a third person. **[Two]**
- Ask for something you want and have not been offered. **[Two]**
- Let somebody else take a job you wanted, and mean it. **[Two]**
- Point out that a decision was made without the person it most affects. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Take the unglamorous job in front of everybody.
- Claim a thing you did that somebody else has been credited for. **[Two] [Think]**

### Rivalry & envy — `standing → resolve, rift`

Wanting what another Maker has. Uncomfortable, universal, and absent from most
NPC repertoires — it is also one of the few reliable sources of self-improvement.

- Read something somebody else made that is better than yours, and work out exactly why. **[Think]**
- Tell somebody their thing is better than yours. **[Two]**
- Go after a claim you know somebody else wants.
- Notice you are competing with somebody who is not competing with you, and decide what to do. **[Think]**
- Take up a subject somebody else considers theirs, and tell them you are doing it. **[Two]**
- Match a standard somebody else set, and say that is what you were doing. **[Two]**

### Alliance & confidence — `bond → accord, debt`

- Back somebody in an argument you were not part of, because you think they are right. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody something you would not say in front of everyone. **[Two]**
- Ask somebody to work on a thing with you rather than beside you. **[Two]**
- Find out where somebody stands on a question you will have to settle later. **[Two] [Think]**
- Keep a confidence, including from somebody who asks you directly. **[Two]**
- Warn somebody you are about to do something they will not like. **[Two]**
- Ask somebody to speak for you somewhere you will not be. **[Two]**

### Trust — `bond → debt, rift`

- Give somebody something of yours to handle, before you are sure of them. **[Two]**
- Take somebody's word for a thing you could check, and do not check it. **[Two]**
- Check a thing somebody told you, and decide whether to tell them you checked. **[Think]**
- Vouch for somebody to a third person, and carry the risk of it. **[Two]**
- Say that you no longer trust somebody's judgement on this, to their face. **[Two] [Think]**
- Give somebody a second chance at a thing they got wrong. **[Two]**

### Withholding & deception — `view → rift`

A cast that cannot keep anything back has no interiority. Included for
completeness; each world decides what its Makers' consciences permit.

- Keep back a thing you know, and be able to say to yourself why. **[Think]**
- Answer narrowly, so what you said is true and the impression is not. **[Two] [Think]**
- Let somebody go on believing a thing about your work that is no longer true. **[Think]**
- Pretend to be further along than you are. **[Two]**
- Own up to a thing you had been quietly not mentioning. **[Two]**
- Decline to answer, and say you are declining rather than deflecting. **[Two]**

### Conflict — `verdict → rift, accord`

- Find whoever disagrees with you and stay with it until one of you has moved. **[Two] [Think]**
- Say that you are being overruled and do not accept the reasoning. **[Two]**
- Refuse to hand over something you are holding, and say what it would take. **[Two]**
- Let an argument go that you could win, because winning would cost more than it is worth. **[Two] [Think]**
- Step between two people getting nowhere and make them say what they actually disagree about. **[Two] [Think]**
- Say out loud that this has stopped being about the work. **[Two]**
- Take a disagreement you cannot settle to somebody who can. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Walk away mid-argument rather than say the thing you are about to say.

### Repair — `rift → bond`

Without it one conflict permanently degrades a relationship and the cast
fragments. The only cluster that consumes `rift`.

- Admit you were wrong about something, to the person you were wrong at. **[Two]**
- Find the person your mistake landed on and make it right with them. **[Two]**
- Go back to somebody you were short with and explain what that was actually about. **[Two]**
- Accept an apology properly, rather than waving it away. **[Two]**
- Ask whether the thing between you is still there, and deal with the answer. **[Two] [Think]**
- Do somebody a favour you owe without mentioning that you owed it. **[Two]**
- Repair a thing you broke rather than apologising for breaking it.

### Long estrangement — `rift → bond, resolve`

- Speak first to somebody you have not properly spoken to in a long time. **[Two] [Think]**
- Work with somebody you fell out with, on something neither of you will give up. **[Two]**
- Say that you have changed your mind about a person, to that person. **[Two]**
- Decide a grudge has outlived its cause and act as though it has. **[Think]**
- Refuse a reconciliation you do not believe in. **[Two]**

### Favours & debts — `debt → bond`

- Ask for help with a thing you could do yourself but would do worse. **[Two]**
- Do something for somebody that is not your job and not owed. **[Two]**
- Repay a favour with what the other person wants rather than what is convenient. **[Two]**
- Say no to a request, say why, and stay on good terms. **[Two]**
- Take on part of somebody's load because they are further behind than you. **[Two]**
- Call in something you are owed, at the moment it will actually help. **[Two]**
- Refuse to let somebody repay you. **[Two]**

### Absent third parties — `bond → standing, finding`

- Ask somebody what they make of a third Maker you both work with. **[Two]**
- Defend somebody who is not here to answer for themselves. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody what a third person is good at, so the next job goes to the right hands. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Repeat something you were told about somebody, having decided it is worth repeating. **[Two] [Think]**
- Decline to repeat something, and say that you are declining. **[Two]**
- Bring two people together who each have half of what the other needs. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Tell somebody what is being said about them. **[Two] [Think]**
- Compare two Makers out loud, to a third. **[Two]**

### Norms — `view → accord`

How a cast decides what is done here. Norm-formation is the difference between a
group and a collection of individuals — and a norm has to persist to be
breakable.

- Say that a thing everyone has been doing should stop. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Propose a way of working and get somebody to agree to it with you. **[Two] [Think]**
- Pull somebody up for cutting a corner everybody cuts. **[Two]**
- Break a rule of this place on purpose and be ready to say why. **[Think]**
- Tell a new Maker the one rule that is not written down anywhere. **[Two]**
- Let a small breach go, and notice that you did.
- Insist on doing a thing properly when nobody would know either way.

### Delegation & authority — `claim → claim, promise`

The cluster that lets a mission be shared out — a claim leaving one Maker and
arriving with another is how a task list becomes several Makers' work.

- Give a piece of your work to somebody and let them do it their way. **[Two]**
- Take a piece of work from somebody and do it their way rather than yours. **[Two]**
- Ask somebody's permission for a thing you could simply have done. **[Two]**
- Decide something that is not yours to decide, because somebody has to. **[Think]**
- Report what you have done to whoever asked, including what went badly. **[Two]**
- Refuse a job you think is wrong, and say what is wrong with it. **[Two] [Think]**
- Argue once, plainly, then do as you were told. **[Two] [Think]**

### Interruption — `· → attention`

- Interrupt somebody deep in something, because what you have cannot wait. **[Two] [Think]**
- Decide not to interrupt, and come back later. **[Think]**
- Be interrupted and give the person your attention properly rather than half of it. **[Two]**
- Be interrupted and say you cannot deal with it now, and when you can. **[Two]**
- Get back into a thing you were pulled out of, and notice what you lost.
- Make yourself visibly unavailable, and hold it.

### Belonging — `· → bond`

- Bring somebody standing on the edge of a conversation into it. **[Two]**
- Notice who has been quiet a long time and go and ask them something. **[Two]**
- Welcome a Maker you have not met and tell them one thing they will need. **[Two]**
- Go and find the one who works alone somewhere else, for no reason but that. **[Two]**
- Leave somebody out of something on purpose, and be able to say why. **[Think]**
- Notice you are the one on the outside of this, and decide what to do about it. **[Think]**

### Support — `bond → bond, resolve`

- Tell somebody whose work was rejected that the work was not the problem, if that is true. **[Two]**
- Mark somebody's finished thing out loud, in front of others. **[Two]**
- Sit with somebody who is stuck without solving it for them. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody you think they are working on the wrong thing. **[Two] [Think]**
- Notice somebody has been at the same problem far too long, and interrupt them. **[Two]**
- Take a thing off somebody who is drowning, without making it a judgement of them. **[Two]**

### Loss — `wake → resolve`

Work destroyed, a Maker gone, a thing retired. A cast with no way to mourn cannot
have anything at stake.

- Tell somebody a thing they made has been retired, and stay while they take it. **[Two]**
- Say what you will miss about a Maker who has gone, to somebody who knew them. **[Two]**
- Read the last thing somebody finished before they left. **[Think]**
- Let go of a piece of your own work you have been defending too long. **[Think]**
- Keep one thing from a body of work being retired, and say why that one. **[Think]**

### Boundaries — `promise → resolve`

- Say you cannot take this on now, and name when you could. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody you need to finish this before you can talk properly. **[Two]**
- Go somewhere nobody will find you and stay there until this is done.
- Take back a promise you should not have made. **[Two]**
- Say that a thing somebody asked is not a thing you will do. **[Two]**

### Play — `bond → bond`

- Tease somebody about a habit of theirs, gently enough that it lands as affection. **[Two]**
- Say the absurd version of what you are both doing, out loud. **[Two]**
- Set somebody a small impossible problem and see what they do with it. **[Two]**
- Take an old joke between you and continue it. **[Two]**
- Make something for no reason except that it amused you. **[Think]**
- Be the one who does not find it funny, and say so. **[Two]**

### Teaching — `standing → standing, claim`

- Show somebody how you do the thing you are best at, in enough detail that they could do it. **[Two] [Think]**
- Correct somebody's work so they can do it themselves next time. **[Two] [Think]**
- Ask somebody to teach you the part you avoid because you are bad at it. **[Two]**
- Give a newer Maker real work and stay near enough to catch it. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Say what you would do differently if you were starting your last piece again. **[Two] [Think]**
- Watch somebody work without helping, and afterwards say what you saw. **[Two] [Think]**
- Take up somebody else's method for a while, properly, and see what it teaches you. **[Think]**

### Disclosure — `view → bond`

- Tell somebody what you are actually worried about in this work. **[Two] [Think]**
- Admit you do not understand something everyone assumes you do. **[Two]**
- Say you have lost confidence in a thing you made and have been leaving it alone. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- Tell somebody why this subject matters to you more than the others. **[Two]**
- Say that you were wrong about a person. **[Two]**
- Tell somebody the thing you have never told anybody here. **[Two] [Think]**

### Face — `view → standing`

Every encounter has a picture of each person in it that both are quietly
maintaining, and most of the work of being sociable is protecting somebody
else's. This is the cluster that makes a room feel occupied by people rather than
by processes — the Maker who saves a colleague from looking foolish has done
something no ledger records.

- Somebody has just said something that made them look worse than they are; give them a way to have meant something better. **[Two] [Think]**
- Take a position out loud that you will be held to, and notice who noticed. **[Two] [Seeds]**
- You are about to be proved wrong in front of others; find the version of being wrong you can live with, and say that one first. **[Two] [Think]**
- Somebody contradicted you in company — work out whether they were correcting the claim or lowering you, and answer the one they meant. **[Two] [Think]**
- Watch somebody overreach in front of a group, and decide whether letting it stand costs them more than the correction would. **[Two] [Think]**
- Praise somebody for the thing they are quietly least sure of. **[Two]**
- Cover for somebody's mistake without either of you naming it, and see whether they let you. **[Two]**
- You have been given more credit than you are owed, in front of people who matter; decide what to do about it before the moment passes. **[Two] [Think]**
- Find the person whose good opinion you have been managing rather than earning, and stop managing it. **[Two] [Think]**
- Ask for something in a way that lets the other refuse without losing anything. **[Two]**

- Find the scene you are proudest of, and check whether removing it changes anything downstream. **[Think]**
- Take the ending you intend, and work backwards to the first moment it became inevitable. **[Think]**
- Find the point of view your piece is worst from, and consider writing it from there. **[Think]**

### Revision — `draft → draft`

Revision has an order, and taking the passes out of order wastes the work:
whether anything is at stake, then where it drags, then the words. Polishing a
scene that should not exist is the commonest way to lose a week.

- Read a draft once for whether anything is at stake, and change nothing else until that is fixed. **[Think]**
- Read it again only for where it drags and where it rushes, and cut from the parts you like. **[Think]**
- Read it a third time for the speech alone, and remove every line that says what the scene has already shown. **[Think]**
- Find the sentence that tells the reader what to feel, and replace it with the thing that would make them feel it. **[Think]**
- Find the vaguest noun in a piece and replace it with a specific one.
- Read a draft aloud, and mark every place your own attention went elsewhere.
- Cut your favourite sentence, and see whether anybody notices. **[Two]**
- Put a draft away long enough to forget it, then read it as a stranger. **[Think]**

## What the vault had to grow

Tasks need somewhere to happen. Four of the clusters above had no room to happen
in, so the building was extended: **eight new fixtures across three levels**,
each carrying the tools its cluster spends. The map is a node graph — rooms open
*off* passages and the loader derives the doors at both ends — so adding a room
is adding a node and its `off`, and the parts standing in it.

Both copies of the map carry the change: `npc-map/maps/` in this repository and
the deployed one under `<mind>/map/battle-cities/`.

| Room | Level, off | Parts | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| The sorting room | `vault-chronicle`, lower cross run | appraisal bench, 2 seats | `record.appraise`, `record.record_reason`, `record.let_go` |
| The catalogue | `vault-chronicle`, lower cross run | catalogue, 2 chronicle terminals | `record.describe`, `record.read_description`, `record.arrange` |
| The mending room | `vault-chronicle`, west run | mending bench | `record.mend`, `record.mark_repair`, `record.read_condition` |
| The board room | `vault-story`, lower cross run | structure board, 6 seats | `structure.lay_out_scenes`, `structure.test_the_want`, `structure.find_the_slack` |
| The receiving room | `vault-command`, cross run | accession desk *(binds the intake)*, stores | `record.accession`, `record.write_provenance`, `record.trace_custody`, `stores.put_back`, `stores.take_out`, `stores.walk_the_racks` |
| The enquiry room | `vault-command`, south run | enquiry desk *(binds an open enquiry)*, 4 seats | `enquiry.take_question`, `enquiry.answer_from_record`, `enquiry.name_the_gap` |

### The command level is no longer work-free

It was authored as the one level nothing happens on. Intake and enquiry *are*
work, so its summary and its `lacks` line now say *no making* rather than *no
work* — and the capacity invariant in `npc-map/tests/vault.rs` that guarded it
was rewritten to say what it actually protects: single posts, never banks of
desks, so no standing order can queue the crew behind them.

The map's own tests judged the change, and caught two defects before either
reached a Maker: a catalogue clause written as a sentence fragment, and the
invalidated capacity check.

**None of the 24 new tool names are implemented in the engine yet.** They are
authored into the parts, so they appear in a Maker's reach and refuse. That is
invariant 8 working as intended, and it is a real edge before missions are wired
to these clusters.

## Engine notes

Not conditions on the list — the tasks stand whether or not these are solved, and
augmenting them later is the plan. These are what the taxonomy implies the engine
will eventually hold, collected so the shape is visible rather than discovered
one task at a time. Most of them are simply *the currencies, made durable*.

1. **A Maker's own finished work** — the `filed` currency, read back. Referenced by every "your last creation" task and all of *Taste*.
2. **Decomposition of a claim** — the `plan` currency. `AuthoredStrategy.parent_id` already models the tree; nothing writes it.
3. **Which held thing is current** — `persona::intent` takes the first active strategy, which is arbitrary rather than chosen.
4. **Size and elapsed effort** — needed to estimate, to pace, and for a duration column to mean anything.
5. **A quality bar** — without it a Maker files immediately or polishes forever, and both look like working.
6. **Dependency between parts of the record** — the `wake` currency. Nothing knows whose work a moved boundary breaks.
7. **Accumulated expertise** — the `standing` currency. "Find whoever would know" has no answer while the cast is interchangeable.
8. **Why a decision went the way it did** — settlements evaporate, so the same argument recurs with no memory of the first.
9. **Claim expiry** — a held thing shows forever; the defect a `wait_for` has without its patience.
10. **Blocked on a decision, not a person** — waiting on somebody is expressible now; waiting on an unmade decision is not.
11. **Time of day, fatigue, pacing** — a day exists but nothing varies with it, which is why routines have little to attach to.
12. **Relationship history, not temperature** — the `bond` and `rift` currencies. *Long estrangement*, *Trust* and *Play* all need what happened between two Makers.
13. **Promises and their due time** — the `promise` currency, the only thing that lets a mission span more than one meeting.
14. **Norms as state** — a norm agreed has to persist to be breakable.
15. **Reputation** — deference, credit and rivalry all presuppose a cast-wide sense of who is good at what.
16. **What has been tried and failed** — a reclaimed job starts from nothing, and *Experiment* depends on failures being kept.
17. **Cross-discipline routing** — a story needing a place needs a Maker working elsewhere; nothing carries the need but a walk.
18. **Assignment** — *Seeds* tasks produce `finding`. Nothing turns a finding into a claim held by a named Maker.
19. **Mood** — the modulation dials exist. Nothing in the loop reads them, so *Self-knowledge* has nothing to notice.
20. **Interruptibility** — the *Interruption* cluster needs a Maker to be in a state that can be broken into.
21. **The creator's counterpart** — presenting finished work implies somebody to present to. Nothing defines what happens when there is nobody.
22. **An enquiry source** — *Service* needs questions to arrive from outside the vault. Nothing produces them.
23. **Provenance as a field** — the `custody` currency. Nothing records where a made thing came from.
24. **Emotional energy** — the `energy` currency. *What an encounter leaves* needs an encounter to have cost or paid something.

## Where this came from

The list was washed against four bodies of practice, each of which contributed
clusters it did not have:

| Source | Contributed |
|---|---|
| The sociology of face-to-face interaction — face-work, lines, interaction ritual chains, emotional energy | *Face*, *What an encounter leaves*, *Bearing* |
| The craft of scene construction — scene as goal/conflict/disaster, sequel as reaction/dilemma/decision, the photographable-goal test, ordered revision passes | *Structure*, *Revision*, and invariant 6 |
| Life aboard a habitat that runs itself — daily planning conferences, weekly housekeeping, routine inspection, the six-monthly full-interior survey for people who have never been inside | *The set-aside day*, *Rounds*, *Fresh eyes* |
| The working functions of an archive — appraisal, accessioning, provenance, arrangement and description, conservation, reference service | *Appraisal*, *Custody*, *Description*, *Condition*, *Service* |

Archival practice was the deepest gap. The list had no concept of *deciding what
is not worth keeping*, no chain of custody, no finding aids, and — most
consequentially — nothing that faced outward. Nobody ever asked the vault a
question.
