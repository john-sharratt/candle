# Sweep — RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell 72 GB, 2026-10-06 (after the origin/main merge)

One full sweep — fourteen width gates and three engine probes, one `cargo` process
per model, card to itself (zend stopped) — on `more-optimizations` after merging
`origin/main` at `80a31ac9`. Since the 2026-10-05 sweep
(`sweep_rtx_pro_5000_72gb_2026-10-05.md`) this build carries:

- main's decode-graph work: each forward recorded as a chain of graphs, wave-aware
  expert movement, the uniform `BatchedInference` sweep recorded a segment per layer;
- the KV compaction sweep made copy-on-write and walked once per substrate, view
  borrow and free run across layers, the reprojection swap cap, and the persistence
  lock that lets foreground callers ahead of segment maintenance.

Single runs, so a gap under ~5% is noise. Every gate row validated, every session.

  decode moved most (Qwen2-0.5B ×1.9, Qwen3.5-0.8B ×1.6), where a forward is short
