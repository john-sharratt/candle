Against the engine row of 2026-10-10 (156.6 / 152.5 / 141.6 decode, 6,019.5 /
5,397.9 / 4,776.9 prefill): decode +7.5% at 4K and +4.3% at 32K, −11.5% at 128K;
prefill −3.7% / −3.1% / −14.9%. The fastest single run, 181.9 t/s at 4K on the
forward bench, is above Strata's median there; the medians are 82–95% of Strata's at
4K and 32K and 69–76% at 128K.

The 128K prompt prefills in three passes (54,000 / 41,625 / 32,375 rows from depths
0 / 54,000 / 95,625). Before the harness fix it failed: the sliced prefill left the
logical offset at 0 between passes, so the second pass was priced from depth 0 — a
42,992 MiB tier for a pass that placed 57,280 MiB — and was refused 1,795,162,112 B
short with the weight zone at its floor.
