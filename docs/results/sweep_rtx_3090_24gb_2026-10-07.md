## What changed since the reference

The commits between `5776799ac` and `7aecbeab3` that move these figures on this card:

- `41bc6be5a` — the uniform `BatchedInference` sweep records CUDA graphs, a segment per
  layer (Qwen2, both Llamas, Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-30B-A3B).

  concession keeps the hottest experts (every routed Qwen3.5/3.6 model).
- `90fc6c304` — the DeltaNet prefill state pass reads four columns per shared load;
  Qwen3.6-35B drafts to depth 4 up to eight sessions.
- `85d8a68f7` — the staging scratch records in place inside a wave capture, and the dense
  Qwen3.5 sweep cuts a segment per layer (0.8B, 9B, 27B).
- `bf573b190`, `1ad5239d9` — Flash-Next decode work: split-K dense reduction, verify
  attention split cap, stacked MoE input projections, faster routing.
- `7aecbeab3` — compaction relocates only the arenas that hold the frontier (the probes).

## Effects to read the ladders against

- **Light-load clock windows.** Single- and two-session configs on the small models can
  run with every kernel 1.4–2× slower for a stretch of configs, then recover at the next
  heavy config — the GPU lowering its clocks under a load that leaves it idle between
  launches. Here: Llama-3.2-3B's F32 ×1 and F16 ×1 (86–88 t/s against 179.7 / 178.8 on
  the same build outside the window), and Qwen3.5-0.8B's C2–C7 ×2 (367–478 against
  614–623 for C0–C1). Neither is a code path; both move between runs.
- **Early rejection on the 10-token hybrid gates.** At draft depth 4 a single-session row
  whose drafter rejects an early proposal has three steps to amortise it in: C5 and C7 ×1
  on the auto hybrid (137.9, 153.2) and C4, C6, C7 ×1 on the Performance one (157–160),
  against 205–227 for their neighbours. The 256-token stock gate shows no such rows.
