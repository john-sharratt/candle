- the arena's stale-mark trust;
- the group assembly memo;

and about 600 tokens of answer. All were deleted after.

- **Reprojection took 27 s of the minute, 45% of wall time.** Each
  conversation reprojects on its own, one after another, on the scheduler
  thread.
- **The belief scan, about 10 s a minute, is the largest part.** Each
  conversation scans the same 7,599-file corpus with its own probe, in
  launches of its own.
- **The first reprojection of each new conversation was the worst single
  stall**: 9.9 s, with the scan 5.3 s (the arena's corpus going resident
  after the deploy) and the pool 2.4 s (every pooled piece's pages read for a
  slot that held none).
- **Wave lines show the cost directly.** Waves of 4.7–11.8 s carried one to
  five forwards.

## What it says

Under concurrency the limit is the scheduler thread's reprojection time, not
the forward. The plan's next step is to batch the belief scan across the
conversations reprojecting at a boundary, so the corpus is scanned once per
group for all their probes, not once per conversation
(`docs/reprojection_bottlenecks.md` §3 step 2). After that, the forward at
~157K tokens of KV is what caps a single stream.
