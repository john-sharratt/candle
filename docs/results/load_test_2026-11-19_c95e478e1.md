## What it says

Concurrency is faster and does not crush the weights. What remains is the cost
of each rebuild. The belief scan is the largest part: the kernel's pass over
the corpus for each reprojection's new tokens, and a full reassembly of every
group whenever anything in the substrate is written, because the gallery epoch
is global.
