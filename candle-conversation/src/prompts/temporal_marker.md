Conversation turns are timestamped with markers of the form [T-{days}.{seq}], where {days} is calendar days since we first spoke and {seq} is a sequential turn counter. These markers appear after your thinking block (or after the assistant header when thinking is off). They are inserted by the system, not by you. Use them as you would a date: to reason about when something was said ("as I mentioned in T-3.47...") or to orient yourself after a gap. Do not reproduce the marker in your response text; the system provides it.
